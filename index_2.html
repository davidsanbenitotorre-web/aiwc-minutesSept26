Decision

Download experience update

User feedback, including from Cisco's and Cornerstone's field teams, shows that the "Download Markdown" button is confusing: about 100 Markdown downloads compared with roughly 800 for the PDF. The group agreed to make the PDF the primary call to action and position Markdown as a secondary option for advanced users. This will be implemented next week.

Responsible AI webinar

Update

New date and panel

The webinar has moved from September 30 to October 12. It will run 60 minutes (down from 90), including 10 minutes of Q&A. The final panel includes Cisco, IBM, Indeed, Google, Miami Dade College and the North Carolina Student Success Center. The report launch remains on track for early December.

Content

Refining the questions

The session has two blocks: enterprise governance and higher education. Based on feedback, the team will:

  • Remove "durable skills" from the education question.
  • Use the enterprise view of the entry-level talent gap as the bridge into the education block.
  • Move the education-side skills question into the opening Q&A.
Promotion

Promotion and timeline

The webinar page goes live the week of September 21. It includes registration, the agenda, speakers and member-recommended courses, plus a social media kit for members to share. The first script draft is due by the end of September, and rehearsals are targeted for October 6–9.

Phase 4 Strategy Workshop

Strategy

Defining the 2027 roadmap

The virtual workshop on September 30 (two hours, plus pre-work) will define the 2027 (Phase 4) roadmap. At least one representative per company is expected. The workshop will validate the sprint-based "spotlight" approach and gauge interest in an optional annual flagship deliverable.

Action for members

Pre-reads and member input

Pre-reads will be circulated before the workshop, including one-pagers on the flagship options and a list of candidate 2027 topics. Members should:

  • Flag any missing topics.
  • Indicate breakout interest.
  • Confirm attendance.
